# Puerto Rico — Rules Reference

> Derived from the official Rio Grande Games English rulebook, included in this
> repo as [puerto-rico-rules-en.pdf](puerto-rico-rules-en.pdf) (© 2001 Andreas
> Seyfarth, © 2002 Ravensburger Spieleverlag; Rio Grande Games printing 222992).
> Scope is the base game only — no expansions. This is a structured restatement
> of the game's mechanics and data tables for implementation purposes, not a
> reproduction of the rulebook; consult the PDF for the authoritative text and
> examples.

## 1. Overview & objective

An economic strategy game for **3–5 players**. Each player develops plantations,
staffs them with colonists, constructs buildings, produces goods, and either
sells them at the trading house for doubloons or ships them for victory points.

The game runs about 15 rounds. Each round, every player chooses exactly one
**role card**; the chooser gets that role's private **privilege**, and every
player then takes that role's **action** in clockwise order starting from the
chooser. The player with the most victory points at game end wins.

## 2. Components

| Count | Component | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Player boards | 12 island spaces + 12 city spaces each |
| 1 | Governor card | Marks the round's starting player |
| 8 | Role cards | Settler, Mayor, Builder, Craftsman, Trader, Captain, **and 2 Prospectors** |
| 1 | Game board | Building display, bank, cargo ships, trading house |
| 49 | Buildings | 5 large violet (2 spaces each), 2 each of 12 small violet, 20 production |
| 54 | Doubloons | 46 × 1, 8 × 5 |
| 58 | Island tiles | 8 quarry + 50 plantation |
| 100 | Colonists | Brown wooden markers |
| 50 | Goods barrels | — |
| 5 | Cargo ships | Capacities 4–8 |
| 50 | VP chips | 32 × 1, 18 × 5 (122 points total) |
| 1 each | Colonist ship, trading house | — |

**Plantation tiles:** indigo 12, sugar 11, corn 10, tobacco 9, coffee 8 (= 50),
plus 8 quarries.

**Goods barrels:** indigo 11, sugar 11, corn 10, tobacco 9, coffee 9 (= 50).

## 3. Setup

Values scale with player count:

| | 3 players | 4 players | 5 players |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starting doubloons (each) | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| VP chip pool | 75 points | 100 points | 122 points (all) |
| Colonist supply | 55 | 75 | 95 |
| Colonists on the colonist ship | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| Cargo ship capacities | 4, 5, 6 | 5, 6, 7 | 6, 7, 8 |
| Role cards in play | 6 (no Prospectors) | 7 (one Prospector) | 8 (all) |
| Face-up plantation tiles | 4 | 5 | 6 |

Roles in play = players + 3, so **exactly three role cards go unchosen each
round**. Face-up plantation tiles = players + 1.

Starting plantations — the starting player takes the governor card and an
**indigo** tile; the rest follow clockwise:

| Players | Distribution |
|---|---|
| 3 | 1st indigo, 2nd indigo, 3rd corn |
| 4 | 1st–2nd indigo, 3rd–4th corn |
| 5 | 1st–3rd indigo, 4th–5th corn |

All 8 quarry tiles go face-up in a stack. The remaining plantation tiles are
shuffled into face-down stacks, and the face-up row is dealt from them.

## 4. Round structure

1. The governor chooses a role card, places it in front of them, takes its
   privilege, and performs its action.
2. Each other player performs that role's action in clockwise order.
3. The governor's left neighbour chooses from the remaining role cards, and so
   on, until every player has chosen exactly one role.
4. The governor places **1 doubloon on each of the three unchosen role cards**,
   on top of any already there.
5. All role cards return to the supply and the governor card passes clockwise.

Rules that hold for every role card:

- Doubloons accumulated on a card go to whoever chooses it, in addition to the
  privilege.
- The chooser always acts first; others follow clockwise.
- Choosing a role is mandatory on your turn, but **using its action or privilege
  is optional** — except the Captain's, where loading is compulsory.
- A chosen card is unavailable for the rest of the round.

## 5. The roles

### Settler — place a new plantation

**Privilege:** may take a **quarry** tile instead of a face-up plantation tile.

**Action:** each player in turn takes one face-up plantation tile (not a quarry,
unless they own an occupied Construction Hut) and places it on any empty island
space.

**Last duty:** the settler discards the untaken face-up tiles and draws
players + 1 fresh tiles from the face-down stacks.

- When the face-down stacks run out, the settler places what remains, then
  shuffles the discards into a new face-down stack. If tiles are still short, the
  face-up row stays short in later rounds.
- Where a tile sits on the island is irrelevant, and tiles are never removed.
- A player with all 12 island spaces filled may not take tiles at all.
- With no quarries left in the stack, neither the settler's privilege nor the
  Construction Hut works.

### Mayor — arrival of new colonists

**Privilege:** take one colonist from the **supply** (not the ship) first.

**Action:** starting with the mayor, players take colonists from the colonist
ship one at a time, clockwise, until it is empty. Each player then places their
colonists — new ones and any previously in San Juan — on empty circles on their
plantations, quarries, and buildings, and may freely rearrange colonists already
placed.

**Last duty:** the mayor refills the colonist ship with **one colonist per empty
circle on every player's buildings** (plantation and quarry circles do **not**
count), with a minimum of one per player in the game.

- A tile with at least one colonist is **occupied**; only occupied tiles function.
- Colonists may only be placed during the mayor phase.
- A player may not leave colonists in San Juan while any circle on their board is
  empty — all empty circles must be filled if possible.
- If the colonist supply is empty, the mayor gets no privilege and does not refill
  the ship.

### Builder — build buildings

**Privilege:** pay **1 doubloon less** for the building constructed this phase.

**Action:** each player in turn may buy one building from the display and place
it on an empty city space. A large building needs **two adjacent** empty city
spaces; buildings may be shuffled within the city to make room, but never removed.

- **No player may build more than one building per round**, and may own **only
  one copy of each building**.
- **Quarry discount:** each occupied quarry reduces the cost by 1 doubloon, capped
  by the building's display column — column 1 caps at 1, column 2 at 2, column 3
  at 3, column 4 at 4. **The column equals the building's VP value.**
- The builder's privilege stacks with the quarry discount, but cost never goes
  below 0.
- A builder who does not build gets no privilege.
- A player with no empty city spaces may not build.

Worked example from the rulebook: with 3 occupied quarries, Construction Hut
costs 1, Office 3, Harbor 5, City Hall 7.

### Craftsman — production of goods

**Privilege:** after everyone has produced, take **one additional good** of a kind
just produced.

**Action:** starting with the craftsman, each player in turn takes goods from the
supply according to their production ability.

- **Corn needs no production building** — an occupied corn plantation yields corn
  directly.
- Every other good needs occupied plantations *and* occupied matching production
  building circles. Yield = min(occupied plantations of that kind, occupied
  circles in matching production buildings).
- If a good's supply is exhausted, the player produces none of it.
- A craftsman who produces nothing gets no bonus good.

### Trader — selling goods

**Privilege:** **+1 doubloon** on the sale.

**Action:** each player in turn may sell one good to the trading house for its
listed price. The phase ends when every player has had a turn **or when the
trading house fills**.

**Last duty:** the trader empties the trading house **only if it holds all four
goods**; a partially filled house carries over to the next trader phase.

- The trading house holds **4 goods**.
- It buys **only kinds not already present** — the Office is the exception.
- Prices: **corn 0, indigo 1, sugar 2, tobacco 3, coffee 4**.
- Selling corn for 0 doubloons is legal.
- A trader who does not sell gets no privilege.

### Captain — shipping of goods

**Privilege:** **+1 victory point** on the first load of the phase, once only.

**Action:** starting with the captain, players take turns loading goods onto the
cargo ships. **Loading is compulsory** — a player who can load, must. The phase
continues clockwise, with players taking several turns each, for as long as
anybody can still load.

Loading rules:

- A cargo ship carries **one kind of goods** at a time.
- A kind already on another cargo ship may not be started on a second ship.
- Nothing may be loaded onto a full ship.
- A player loads **one kind per turn**, and must load **as many barrels of it as
  possible**. Where several ships could take that kind, they must pick the one
  that accommodates the most.
- Which *kind* to load is a free choice; a player need not pick the kind that
  ships the most barrels.

**Victory points:** 1 VP per barrel loaded, regardless of kind. VP chips are held
face-down and kept secret.

**Goods storage** (once no more loading is possible): each player keeps **one
barrel** on their windrose, plus whatever their warehouses protect. Everything
else returns to the supply. Players choose what to keep. Storage limits are
checked **only** at the end of the captain phase — goods gained in other phases
accumulate freely until then.

**Last duty:** the captain unloads all **completely full** ships to the supply.
Partly loaded and empty ships keep their state into the next round.

### Prospector — no action

**Privilege:** take **1 doubloon** from the bank. Triggers no action for anyone,
including the chooser. Two Prospector cards exist; both are in play only at 5
players.

## 6. Buildings

Every building shows its cost in the first circle and its VP value in the upper
right. A building scores its VP whether occupied or not; **special functions and
large-building bonuses require occupation**. The number of circles is the
colonist capacity.

### Production buildings (20)

| Building | Cost | VP | Circles | Copies | Produces |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Small Indigo Plant | 1 | 1 | 1 | 4 | Indigo |
| Small Sugar Mill | 2 | 1 | 1 | 4 | Sugar |
| Indigo Plant | 3 | 2 | 3 | 3 | Indigo |
| Sugar Mill | 4 | 2 | 3 | 3 | Sugar |
| Tobacco Storage | 5 | 3 | 3 | 3 | Tobacco |
| Coffee Roaster | 6 | 3 | 2 | 3 | Coffee |

Corn has no production building.

### Small violet buildings (12 types, 2 copies each, 1 circle each)

| Building | Cost | VP | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small Market | 1 | 1 | +1 doubloon per sale (trader phase) |
| Hacienda | 2 | 1 | +1 face-down plantation (settler phase) |
| Construction Hut | 2 | 1 | Quarry instead of a plantation (settler phase) |
| Small Warehouse | 3 | 1 | Store all barrels of 1 chosen kind (captain phase) |
| Hospice | 4 | 2 | +1 colonist when settling (settler phase) |
| Office | 5 | 2 | Sell a kind already in the trading house (trader phase) |
| Large Market | 5 | 2 | +2 doubloons per sale (trader phase) |
| Large Warehouse | 6 | 2 | Store all barrels of 2 chosen kinds (captain phase) |
| Factory | 7 | 3 | Doubloons for variety produced (craftsman phase) |
| University | 8 | 3 | +1 colonist on each building built (builder phase) |
| Harbor | 8 | 3 | +1 VP per loading action (captain phase) |
| Wharf | 9 | 3 | Private ship (captain phase) |

Detail on the ones with non-obvious edges:

- **Hacienda** — before taking a face-up tile, take the top face-down tile and
  place it immediately; it may not be discarded. A player taking the face-down
  tile may not swap it for a quarry, even owning a Construction Hut. A settler
  who owns a Hacienda still takes at most one quarry.
- **Construction Hut** — a settler who owns one still takes at most one quarry.
- **Hospice** — no colonist for the Hacienda's extra tile. If the colonist supply
  is empty, take one from the colonist ship; if that is empty too, go without.
- **Office** — cannot sell into a *full* trading house; the exception is only
  about duplicate kinds.
- **Small + Large Warehouse together** protect 3 kinds (plus the free barrel).
- **Small + Large Market together** pay +3 doubloons per sale.
- **Factory** — by distinct kinds produced this craftsman phase: 1 → 0, 2 → 1,
  3 → 2, 4 → 3, 5 → 5 doubloons. Barrel count is irrelevant.
- **University** — one colonist per building built, regardless of its circles.
  Same supply → colonist ship → nothing fallback as the Hospice.
- **Wharf** — instead of loading a cargo ship, put **all** barrels of one kind
  into the supply and score them as if shipped. Capacity 11 barrels; the kind may
  duplicate one already at sea. Usable **once per captain phase**, at a moment of
  the owner's choosing, and using it is optional even though loading is otherwise
  compulsory. The owner must ship every barrel of the chosen kind, but need not
  choose the kind they hold most of.

### Large violet buildings (5, one copy each)

Each costs **10**, scores **4 VP**, has **1 circle**, and occupies **2 adjacent
city spaces** while counting as one building. Their bonuses apply **only when
occupied**, and are scored at game end.

| Building | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Guild Hall | +1 VP per small production building, +2 VP per large production building (occupied or not) |
| Residence | 4 VP for ≤9 filled island spaces, 5 for 10, 6 for 11, 7 for 12 |
| Fortress | +1 VP per 3 colonists anywhere on the player's board, San Juan included |
| Customs House | +1 VP per 4 VP **chips** earned during play; building VP excluded |
| City Hall | +1 VP per violet building in the city, occupied or not, **counting itself** |

## 7. Game end & scoring

The game ends **at the end of the round** in which any of these occurs:

1. At the end of a mayor phase, there are not enough colonists to refill the
   colonist ship as required.
2. During a builder phase, at least one player builds on their **12th city space**.
3. During a captain phase, the **last VP chip is taken**. Victory points keep
   accruing after that — they are simply tracked on paper.

Final score per player:

- VP chips earned during play (including any tracked after the chips ran out), **+**
- the printed VP of every building in their city, occupied or not, **+**
- the extra VPs of their **occupied** large buildings.

Doubloons and leftover goods score nothing. **Tiebreak:** most doubloons plus
goods combined, counting 1 good as 1 doubloon.

## 8. Glossary

- **Doubloon** — currency; buys buildings, scores nothing directly.
- **Colonist** — worker; occupies a circle on a tile or building to activate it.
- **Occupied** — has at least one colonist. Unoccupied tiles never function.
- **San Juan** — the player-board holding area for unplaced colonists.
- **Island space / city space** — the 12 + 12 slots on a player board for
  plantations/quarries and buildings respectively.
- **Windrose** — the player-board area holding doubloons, goods, and VP chips.
- **Governor** — the round's first chooser; rotates clockwise each round.
- **Privilege** — the bonus available only to the player who chose the role.
